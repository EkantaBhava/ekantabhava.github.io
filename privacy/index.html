Privacy

Applies to every app in the Ekanta Bhava family — Ekanta Yatra, Karma Yatra, Loka Yatra.

What we store, and where

None of it is ever transmitted to us — we don't run servers that receive it, and there's no account tying it to you. What happens beyond that differs slightly by app, so here it is plainly, app by app:

  • Ekanta Yatra stores nothing at all, anywhere. Each reflection happens in the moment, on your device, using Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework — and it's gone once the session ends. Nothing is saved, on your device or off it.
  • Loka Yatra stores what you build — your relational map, the boundaries you've set — locally on your device only. It is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Karma Yatra stores your quests and commitments locally on your device, the same way. For certain complex calculations — when a decision weighs trade-offs across several areas of life at once, more than the on-device model can resolve alone — it may briefly use Apple's Private Cloud Compute to help synthesize the answer. Private Cloud Compute is Apple's own privacy-preserving cloud system: built so that data sent to it is used only for that one request, not retained afterward, not accessible to Apple staff, and independently verifiable by security researchers. It's Apple's architecture, not something we built — we're relying on Apple's own guarantees here, the same way any app using this framework does.

What we don't collect

  • No account or sign-in is required to use any of these apps.
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs are built into them.
  • Nothing you write is read, reviewed, or used to train anything, by us or anyone else.
  • We don't sell or share data, because we don't hold any to begin with.

TestFlight, specifically

If you're trying an app through TestFlight, that installation and update process is run by Apple, under Apple's own privacy terms — not ours. If you choose to send feedback through TestFlight's built-in "Send Beta Feedback," that feedback (and any screenshot or diagnostic you attach) goes to Apple and is shared with us as the developer. That's optional and only happens if you actively send it.

If you email us

Writing to us directly — for feedback, a question, or to join a waitlist — is ordinary email correspondence. We see what you send us, the same as any email, and use it only to respond or to understand what to build next. We don't add you to a marketing list without asking.

Changes

If this ever changes — for instance, if a future feature introduces optional cloud sync — this page will say so plainly, and any such feature will be opt-in, not the default.
